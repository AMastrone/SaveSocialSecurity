Model Validation

The simulator is calibrated against the Social Security Administration's 2026 Trustees Report and compared with reform estimates from SSA's Office of the Chief Actuary. The results below show how closely the simplified projection framework reproduces baseline solvency measures and selected reform effects.

Because the simulator simplifies certain aspects of the Social Security program, not every SSA actuarial projection can be tested directly against the model. The baseline measures and reform estimates that can be meaningfully compared are presented below.
