How far from the target before stabilizers react.
Priority 1 responds after the first consecutive year outside the trust fund ratio deadband. Priority 2 joins after two consecutive years. Priority 3 joins after three. Stabilizers with the same priority respond together. Strength controls determine how large each yearly adjustment can be.
